# Code of Conduct

Status: proposed policy pending owner review.

We expect respectful, inclusive, technically rigorous collaboration. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of customer data or secrets, and disruption of the project are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `office@attentus.pl`. Maintainers may remove content or participation that violates this policy. A final enforcement and appeals policy must be approved before public launch.
