# Open-core boundary

## Community — Apache-2.0

OpenPPWR Community contains the complete self-hosted packaging, supplier,
evidence, assessment, remediation, dossier, audit and EN/PL/DE reference
workflow. Its small demonstration rule pack is transparent, versioned,
non-authoritative and requires human regulatory review.

Attentus-owned Community source, public SDKs and integration contracts, public
documentation, examples and synthetic ACME assets are licensed under Apache
License 2.0. Third-party components and assets keep their respective licenses.
Apache-2.0 grants no rights to OpenPPWR or Attentus trademarks.

## Commercial products — separate terms

- OpenPPWR Cloud operations use commercial SaaS terms, DPA and SLA.
- OpenPPWR Connect production ERP/SAP implementations are separate commercial
  modules or services. Public canonical contracts and SDKs may remain
  Apache-2.0.
- OpenPPWR Regulatory maintained, versioned regulatory content uses commercial
  subscription terms. Community contains only the basic demonstration pack
  needed for its reproducible workflow.
- OpenPPWR Enterprise modules, identity controls, support and private deployment
  capabilities use separate commercial agreements.
- OpenPPWR Services use commercial services agreements.

This repository may contain truthful public descriptions of those boundaries.
Public export must never contain private implementations, operations or
infrastructure; customer configuration or data; maintained commercial rule
packs; confidential pricing, contracts or support material.

Community is not intentionally crippled, and commercial products do not change
rights already granted for Apache-2.0 files. No generic extension framework is
introduced. A stable extension point requires two real consumers or an ADR
explaining unavoidable early standardization.
